How Bluetooth Works

Phone to headphones, through thin air. 🎵 Sound on!

That’s Bluetooth.

  1. Radio waves carry it.
  2. It hops channels to dodge noise.
  3. Gadgets shout “I’m here!”
  4. Pairing makes a secret key.
  5. Data flies as 1s and 0s.
  6. A music backup stops stutters.
